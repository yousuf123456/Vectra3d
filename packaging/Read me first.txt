Vectra3D - read me first
========================

1. Installing. Run Vectra3D-0.1.0-setup.exe and follow the steps; no
   administrator password is needed. Vectra3D is then in the Start menu.
   (If you have the zip instead: right-click it, choose "Extract All...",
   then open the Vectra3D folder and double-click Vectra3D.exe. Keep all
   the other files in the folder next to it.)

2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". The app is not signed, so
   Windows does not recognise it yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

3. Starting takes a while. A small Vectra3D window shows what is loading;
   the first launch can take a minute, later ones are quicker.

4. Allow camera access if Windows asks. For hand tracking, hold your hands
   up with your palms facing the camera, in reasonable light.

5. No camera? Everything still works from the panels on the right: type
   vectors, edit them, and run every operation there.

6. No internet is needed. Pictures from the camera are used only while the
   app is open; they are never saved or sent anywhere.

To remove Vectra3D: Settings > Apps, find Vectra3D, and choose Uninstall.
