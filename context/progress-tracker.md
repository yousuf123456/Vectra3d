| 2026-09-23 | `MAX_COMPONENT` is 1e37, two orders below the float32 ceiling | Headroom that is actually needed: the drawn world reaches about 1.3 extents past the longest vector for arrowheads, letters and the camera, and a resultant can be the sum of ten vectors. The user chose the technical limit over a friendlier number such as 1e6, because any lower figure would be an invented rule about what students may type and would refuse input the app can genuinely draw. |
